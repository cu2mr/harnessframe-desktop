# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

HarnessFrame welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels and viewpoints. We are committed to a respectful, constructive and harassment-free community in project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their merits, with patience and specific feedback.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses and responsible security disclosure.
- Help newcomers participate and make project decisions understandable.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety and community moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, impersonation, doxxing and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post credentials, exploit payloads or sensitive logs in public project spaces.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces where someone represents HarnessFrame or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [angelo.cyj@gmail.com](mailto:angelo.cyj@gmail.com). Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Include enough context for a fair review, but avoid sharing unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions will consider the behavior's impact, pattern and severity. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, and retaliation against reporters or participants in a review is prohibited.
